How a project moves

Five phases, from the first conversation to support after launch. Timing is agreed at scoping, once the scope is clear.

1

Discovery and scope

We agree what the project must do, and what it will not do, before any build starts. You receive a written scope.

2

UX and visual design

We design how the site or app works and looks, and you approve it before we build.

3

Build and integration

We build the approved design and connect the tools it needs.

4

QA and launch

We test the build, fix what fails, and launch when it is ready.

5

Support and improvement

After launch we keep it running and improve it from real use.

Questions

How does a project start?

With a conversation about what you need. We agree the scope in writing before any build starts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. Timing is agreed at scoping, once the scope is clear.

How much does it cost?

No fixed prices are published. Every project is priced from its scope, after the first conversation.

What happens after launch?

We keep the site or app running and improve it from real use. Support is agreed as part of the scope.

Who owns the finished work?

Ownership of the website, app, code and content is agreed in writing before the build starts.

Can you rebuild or improve an existing site or app?

Yes. Tell us what exists today and what is not working, and we will talk through the options.
